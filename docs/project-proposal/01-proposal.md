## 2. Project Description

The proposed project focuses on the design and development of a web-based information system for creating, managing, and visualizing digital profiles of university students.

The system aims to establish a centralized platform that organizes academic information, student achievements, skills, competencies, and educational progress into a unified digital profile.

One of the key features of the proposed solution is the **automated calculation and visualization of academic performance indicators** based on stored student data. Through interactive dashboards, students will be able to monitor their academic progress and review their achievements in a clear and structured manner.

Teachers will have access to relevant academic information, allowing them to manage student records and monitor the performance of students assigned to their courses.

Furthermore, the system will be designed with flexibility and adaptability in mind, making it potentially applicable to different higher education institutions rather than being restricted to a single university.

## 3. Problem Statement

In higher education institutions, student-related information, including academic performance, achievements, and educational progress, may be distributed across multiple information systems.

Such fragmentation can complicate the process of obtaining a comprehensive overview of an individual student's academic development for both students and teachers.

Additionally, existing approaches may provide limited opportunities to integrate academic records, personal achievements, and competencies within a single digital profile. Insufficient visualization capabilities can further complicate the interpretation of academic results and the identification of performance trends over time.

To address these challenges, the proposed project aims to develop a **centralized student digital profile system** that combines academic information management, automated performance calculations, and interactive data visualization.

The relevance of these identified challenges will be further examined through stakeholder discussions and a comparative analysis of existing educational information systems.

## 4. Target Users

The proposed information system is intended for three primary categories of users.

### 4.1. Students

Students will be able to access their personal digital profiles, review academic results, monitor educational progress, and manage selected personal achievements.

### 4.2. Teachers

Teachers will be able to enter and update academic results, access information about students assigned to their courses, and analyze individual academic performance.

### 4.3. System Administrators

System administrators will be responsible for managing user accounts, assigning access permissions, organizing student groups, and maintaining essential system configurations.

## 5. Project Aim and Objectives

### 5.1. Project Aim

The primary aim of this project is to design and develop a web-based information system that facilitates the creation, management, and visualization of student digital profiles, enabling academic progress monitoring and structured access to educational information.

### 5.2. Project Objectives

To achieve this aim, the following objectives have been established:

1. Analyze existing educational information systems and approaches to student profile management.
2. Identify and document the functional and non-functional requirements of the proposed system.
3. Design a modular system architecture and an appropriate database structure.
4. Implement user authentication and role-based access control mechanisms.
5. Develop functionality for creating, viewing, and managing student digital profiles.
6. Implement academic record management and automated GPA calculations.
7. Develop interactive dashboards and visualizations of student academic performance.
8. Provide functionality for managing selected student achievements and certificates.
9. Evaluate the developed system through functional testing, data accuracy verification, and usability assessment.

## 6. Expected Outcomes

The successful completion of the project is expected to produce the following outcomes:

- **Functional Web Application:** A working Student Digital Profile Information System accessible through a web interface.
- **Structured Database:** A database designed to store and manage student-related and academic information.
- **Role-Based Access Control:** Authentication and authorization mechanisms supporting students, teachers, and system administrators.
- **Automated Academic Calculations:** Functionality for calculating GPA and selected academic performance indicators.
- **Interactive Data Visualization:** Dashboards and graphical representations illustrating academic progress and performance trends.
- **Achievements Management:** Functionality for adding, storing, and viewing selected student achievements and certificates.
- **Technical Documentation:** Documentation covering system architecture, requirements, design decisions, and implementation.
- **Testing and Evaluation Results:** Evidence demonstrating the system's functionality, accuracy, and compliance with the defined requirements.

## 7. Project Scope

The project scope is divided into three categories: functionality included in the initial Minimum Viable Product (MVP), potential future enhancements, and features excluded from the initial implementation.

### 7.1. In Scope — Initial MVP

The initial version of the system will focus on implementing essential functionality required to create, manage, and visualize student digital profiles.

#### A. User Management

- User authentication using test accounts.
- Role-based access control for students, teachers, and administrators.
- Restricted access allowing teachers to manage only their assigned students.

#### B. Student Digital Profile

- Storage and display of basic student information.
- Management of academic records and enrolled courses.
- Presentation of academic performance indicators.
- Management of selected student achievements and certificates.

#### C. Academic Performance Management

- Functionality for teachers to enter and update academic results.
- Storage of student grades and course credits.
- Automated GPA calculation based on configurable grading rules.

#### D. Data Visualization

- An academic dashboard displaying essential student indicators.
- Graphical representations of GPA and academic progress.
- Visualization of academic performance across different semesters.
- Presentation of basic academic statistics.

### 7.2. Planned Future Enhancements

Following the implementation of the initial MVP, the system may be extended with additional functionality, including:

- Advanced management of student skills and competencies.
- Digital portfolios containing academic and personal projects.
- Student attendance tracking.
- Recording of extracurricular activities.
- Exporting student digital profiles in PDF format.
- Importing student information from CSV and Excel files.
- Integration with external university information systems.
- Full multilingual support in Kazakh, Russian, and English.
- Advanced academic analytics and reporting capabilities.

These enhancements will be considered based on development progress, available resources, and stakeholder feedback.

### 7.3. Out of Scope

The following features are excluded from the initial implementation:

- Development of a native mobile application.
- Replacement of existing university Learning Management Systems (LMS).
- Direct integration with external university databases without authorized access.

## 8. Project Constraints and Assumptions

The project will be developed by a three-member team, with responsibilities distributed across backend development, frontend development, and system analysis and quality assurance.

The proposed solution will initially be implemented as a **web-based application**, with an emphasis on modular architecture and opportunities for future expansion.

Since access to official university information systems and databases is currently unavailable, the initial prototype will rely on synthetic or otherwise authorized test data.

Academic performance calculations will follow documented and configurable grading rules to support potential adaptation to the evaluation systems of different universities.

The project scope, functional requirements, and implementation priorities may be refined based on recommendations and feedback provided by the academic supervisor.

## 9. Verification and Evaluation Approach

The developed system will be evaluated to determine whether it satisfies the defined functional requirements and achieves its intended objectives.

The evaluation will focus on the following aspects:

- **Data Accuracy:** Verification of correct storage, processing, and retrieval of student information.
- **Calculation Correctness:** Validation of automated academic performance calculations, including GPA.
- **Visualization Accuracy:** Verification of consistency between stored academic data and the information presented in dashboards and charts.
- **Access Control:** Assessment of whether user roles and permissions are enforced correctly.
- **System Performance:** Measurement of application response times under defined testing conditions.
- **Usability:** Collection and analysis of feedback from students and teachers regarding the system's usability and clarity.

A detailed testing methodology, evaluation metrics, and measurable acceptance criteria will be established in the separate Verification and Evaluation Plan.
